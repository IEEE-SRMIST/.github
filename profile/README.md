## What we do

<details>
<summary><b>Hackathons</b>: build something real in a day or two</summary>
<br>
HackTrix (24 hours), TechTrek (48 hours, e-commerce websites) and Protocol (online, five days) bring teams together to design, code and ship under a deadline.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Concepto</b>: our yearly flagship</summary>
<br>
Concepto takes a new shape every year: a speaker series, ideathons where teams pitch their ideas, and a UI/UX workshop.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Workshops and talks</b>: learn from people who do it every day</summary>
<br>
Hands-on sessions on cloud (HELLO CLOUD!), blockchain (Hyperforge) and generative AI (EVOLVE).
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Tech fests</b>: two days of everything</summary>
<br>
TechnoZest, our two-day Spider-Verse themed tech fest, brought competitions, talks and workshops together.
</details>
